Example 3: Find Measures in Intersecting Circles

The diameter of $\odot S$ is $30$ units, the diameter of $\odot R$ is $20$ units, and $DS = 9$ units. Find $CD$.

Because the diameter of $\odot S$ is $30$, $CS = 15$. $\overline{CD}$ is part of radius $\overline{CS}$.

$CD +$ $DS$ $= $ $CS$
Segment Addition Postulate
$CD + 9 = $ $15$
Substitution
$CD = 6$
Subtract 9 from each side.
