Example 1

Write the Equation of a Parabola

Write an equation for each parabola.

a. focus at (0, 1) and directrix y = −1

The directrix is horizontal, so the axis of symmetry is vertical. The equation is of the form (x − h)2 = 4p(y − k).

Use the focus and directrix to find the values of h, k, and p.

The focus is (h, k + p) = (0, 1), so h = 0 and k + p = 1.

The directrix is y = k − p, so k − p = −1. Solve the system to find k and p.

k + p = 1
k − p = −1
2k = 0
k = 0
k + p = 1
0 + p = 1
p = 1

Substitute to find the equation.

(x − h)2 = 4p(y − k)
(x − 0)2 = 4(1)(y − 0)
x2 = 4y

The equation is x2 = 4y or y = ¼x2.
