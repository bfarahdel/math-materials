Example 1
Angle of Elevation

VACATION Leah wants to see a castle in an amusement park. She sights the top of the castle at an angle of elevation of 38°. She knows that the castle is 190 feet tall. If Leah is 5.5 feet tall, how far is she from the castle to the nearest foot?

Step 1:

Make a sketch to represent the situation.

Because Leah is 5.5 feet tall, BC = 190 − 5.5 or 184.5 feet. Let x represent the distance from Leah to the castle, AC.

Formula:
tan A =
BC AC
Substitute:
tan 38° =
184.5 x
Rearrange:
x =
184.5 tan 38°
Calculate:
x ≈ 236.1
→
Trig Ratio: tan = opposite adjacent
→
Substitute knowns:

m∠A = 38, BC = 184.5, AC = x

→
Solve for x:

Cross-multiply and isolate the unknown variable.

→
Use a calculator:

Ensure your scientific or graphing calculator is set to Degree mode.

Leah is about 236 feet from the castle.
