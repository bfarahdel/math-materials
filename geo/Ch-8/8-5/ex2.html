Example 2

Angle of Depression

EMERGENCY A search and rescue team is airlifting people from the scene of a boating accident when they observe another person in need of help. If the angle of depression to this other person is 42° and the helicopter is 18 feet above the water, what is the horizontal distance from the rescuers to this person to the nearest foot?

Step-by-step Solution

Make a sketch of the situation.

Because AB and DC are parallel, m∠BAC = m∠ACD by the Alternate Interior Angles Theorem.
