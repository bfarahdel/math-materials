Modeling: Disco Ball Height

Problem: Annabelle and Rich are setting up decorations. Rich is standing 5 feet directly in front of Annabelle under a disco ball. The angle of elevation from Annabelle is 40°, and from Rich is 50°. How high is the disco ball?

Step-by-Step Solution

1. Define variables: Let x be the height of the ball, d be the distance from Rich to the point directly under the ball.

2. Set up equations using trigonometry:

  • For Rich: tan(50°) = x / d → d = x / tan(50°)
  • For Annabelle: tan(40°) = x / (d + 5)

3. Substitute and Solve:

tan(40°) = x / ( (x / tan(50°)) + 5 )
x ≈ 11.95 feet

The disco ball is approximately 11.95 feet high.
