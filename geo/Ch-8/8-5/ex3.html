Lenora's Bike Ramp Problem

Lenora wants to build a backyard bike ramp. She knows the height needs to be 10 feet to clear safety specifications, and the slope requires an angle of elevation of 20°. How long should the base of the ramp be?

Step 1: Choose the Right Trigonometric Function

Look at our triangle. Relative to our angle of elevation ($20^\circ$):

  • Opposite Side = 10 ft
  • Adjacent Side = Unknown (base)

Which formula connects Opposite and Adjacent?

Step 2: Set up the Equation

Correct! We use the **tangent** ratio because it directly compares the height (Opposite) with the base (Adjacent). Let's plug in our known values into the template:

$\tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}$
