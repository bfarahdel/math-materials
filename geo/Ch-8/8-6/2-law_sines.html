Use the Law of Sines to Solve Triangles

You can use the area formulas to derive the Law of Sines, which shows the relationships between side lengths of a triangle and the sines of the angles opposite them.

$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{red}{A} = \frac{1}{2}\color{orange}{a}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{blue}{B} = \frac{1}{2}\color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b} \color{black}{\sin} \color{green}{C}$
Set the area formulas equal to each other.
$\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{red}{A} = \color{orange}{a}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{blue}{B} = \color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b} \color{black}{\sin} \color{green}{C}$
Multiply each expression by 2.
$\frac{\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{red}{A}}{\color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c}} = \frac{\color{orange}{a}\color{purple}{c} \color{black}{\sin} \color{blue}{B}}{\color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c}} = \frac{\color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b} \color{black}{\sin} \color{green}{C}}{\color{orange}{a}\color{teal}{b}\color{purple}{c}}$
Divide each expression by $abc$.
$\frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{red}{A}}{\color{orange}{a}} = \frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{blue}{B}}{\color{teal}{b}} = \frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{green}{C}}{\color{purple}{c}}$
Simplify.
🧩 Key Concept: Law of Sines

In $\triangle ABC$, if sides with lengths $\color{orange}{a}$, $\color{teal}{b}$, and $\color{purple}{c}$ have opposite angles with measures $\color{red}{A}$, $\color{blue}{B}$, and $\color{green}{C}$, respectively, then the following is true.

$\frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{red}{A}}{\color{orange}{a}} = \frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{blue}{B}}{\color{teal}{b}} = \frac{\color{black}{\sin} \color{green}{C}}{\color{purple}{c}}$
