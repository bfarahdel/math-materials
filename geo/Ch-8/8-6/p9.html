Determine whether each ΔABC has no solution, one solution, or two solutions. Then solve the triangle.

9. A = 60°, a = 15, b = 24

Solution:

In ΔABC, we have an Angle-Side-Side (SSA) case: ∠A = 60°, a = 15, and b = 24.

1. Check for solution existence (Law of Sines):

sin(B) b
=
sin(A) a
sin(B) 24
=
sin(60°) 15
sin(B) =
24 × sin(60°) 15
sin(B) =
24 × 0.866 15
≈ 1.386

Since sin(B) must be between -1 and 1, and 1.386 > 1, this value is impossible.

Results:

There is no solution for this triangle.
