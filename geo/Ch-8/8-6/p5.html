Solve each triangle. Round side lengths to the nearest tenth and angle measures to the nearest degree.

Solution:

In ΔDEF, we are given: ∠D = 39°, ∠F = 34°, and side FD = 12.

1. Find ∠E:

∠E = 180° - (39° + 34°) = 180° - 73° = 107°

2. Find side f (opposite ∠F=34°):

Using Law of Sines:

sin(E) FD
=
sin(D) f

sin(107°) / 12 = sin(39°) / f

f = (12 × sin(39°)) / sin(107°) ≈ 7.9

3. Find side d (opposite ∠D=39°):

Using Law of Sines:

sin(E) FD
=
sin(F) d

sin(107°) / 12 = sin(34°) / d

d = (12 × sin(34°)) / sin(107°) ≈ 7.0

Results:

∠E = 107°, f ≈ 7.9, d ≈ 7.0
