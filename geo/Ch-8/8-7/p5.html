Problem 5

Solving a triangle using the Law of Cosines and the Law of Sines.

The Problem

Given a triangle with sides b = 12, a = 8, and included angle A = 107° (where A is actually the angle between sides c and b, let's designate the unknown side opposite 107° as a').

Calculation

1. Find side a (opposite the 107° angle) using the Law of Cosines: \( a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cdot \cos(A) \)

$$a^2 = 12^2 + 8^2 - 2(12)(8) \cdot \cos(107^{\circ})$$ $$a^2 = 144 + 64 - 192 \cdot (-0.2924)$$ $$a^2 = 208 + 56.14 = 264.14$$ $$a = \sqrt{264.14} \approx 16.25$$

2. Use Law of Sines to find angle B:

$$\frac{\sin(B)}{12} = \frac{\sin(107^{\circ})}{16.25}$$ $$\sin(B) = \frac{12 \cdot 0.9563}{16.25} \approx 0.7065$$ $$B = \sin^{-1}(0.7065) \approx 44.95^{\circ} \approx 45^{\circ}$$

3. Find angle C:

$$C = 180^{\circ} - (107^{\circ} + 45^{\circ}) = 28^{\circ}$$

Final Results:

\( a \approx 16.25, \quad B \approx 45^{\circ}, \quad C \approx 28^{\circ} \)
