Example 2: Solve a Triangle Given Three Sides

Solve ΔABC.

Step 1

Use the Law of Cosines to find the measure of the largest angle, ∠A.

a² = b² + c² - 2bc cos A
16² = 9² + 10² - 2(9)(10) cos A a=16, b=9, c=10
16² - 9² - 10² = -2(9)(10) cos A Subtract 9² and 10²
(16² - 9² - 10²) / -2(9)(10) = cos A Divide by -2(9)(10)
-0.4167 ≈ cos A Simplify
115° ≈ A Use cos⁻¹
Step 2

Use the Law of Sines to find the measure of ∠B.

(sin B) / 9 ≈ (sin 115°) / 16
sin B ≈ (9 sin 115°) / 16 Multiply by 9
sin B ≈ 0.5098 Calculate
B ≈ 31° Use sin⁻¹
Step 3

Find the measure of ∠C.

m∠C ≈ 180° - (115° + 31°) or about 34°

So, A ≈ 115°, B ≈ 31°, and C ≈ 34°.
