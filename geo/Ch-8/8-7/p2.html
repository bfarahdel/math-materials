Problem 2

Solving a triangle with three known sides using the Law of Cosines.

The Problem

Given a triangle with sides a = 14, b = 10, and c = 20, find the measures of the interior angles.

Calculation

Using the Law of Cosines: \( \cos(A) = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc} \)

$$\cos(A) = \frac{10^2 + 20^2 - 14^2}{2(10)(20)} = \frac{100 + 400 - 196}{400} = \frac{304}{400} = 0.76$$ $$A = \cos^{-1}(0.76) \approx 40.5^\circ \rightarrow \mathbf{41^\circ}$$ $$\cos(B) = \frac{14^2 + 20^2 - 10^2}{2(14)(20)} = \frac{196 + 400 - 100}{560} = \frac{496}{560} \approx 0.886$$ $$B = \cos^{-1}(0.886) \approx 27.6^\circ \rightarrow \mathbf{28^\circ}$$

Final Angles:

\( A \approx 41^\circ, \quad B \approx 28^\circ \)

\( C = 180^\circ - (41^\circ + 28^\circ) = 111^\circ \)
