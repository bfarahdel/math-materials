Problem 1

Solving a triangle with $SAS$ (Side-Angle-Side) using the Law of Cosines and Law of Sines.

The Problem

Given a triangle with sides $a = 3$, $c = 4$, and an included angle $B = 92^\circ$, find the measures of the third side $b$ and the remaining angles $A$ and $C$.

Calculation

1. Find side $b$ using the Law of Cosines: $b^2 = a^2 + c^2 - 2ac \cdot \cos(B)$

$$b^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 - 2(3)(4) \cdot \cos(92^\circ)$$ $$b^2 = 9 + 16 - 24 \cdot (-0.0349)$$ $$b^2 = 25.8376$$ $$b = \sqrt{25.8376} \approx \mathbf{5.1}$$

2. Find angle $A$ using the Law of Sines: $\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b}$

$$\sin A = \frac{3 \cdot \sin(92^\circ)}{5.083} \approx 0.5902$$ $$A = \arcsin(0.5902) \approx 36.16^\circ \rightarrow \mathbf{36^\circ}$$

3. Find angle $C$ using the angle sum property:

$$C = 180^\circ - (92^\circ + 36.16^\circ) = 51.84^\circ \rightarrow \mathbf{52^\circ}$$

Final Results:

$b \approx 5.1, \quad A \approx 36^\circ, \quad C \approx 52^\circ$
