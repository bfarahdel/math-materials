Problem 1

Using trigonometric ratios to find unknown side lengths.

The Problem

Given a right-angled triangle with an angle of 30° and an opposite side of 5 meters, find the length of the adjacent side.

Calculation

Using the tangent ratio:

$$\tan(30^\circ) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}$$ $$\tan(30^\circ) = \frac{5}{x}$$ $$x = \frac{5}{\tan(30^\circ)}$$

Result:

\( x \approx 8.66 \text{ meters} \)
