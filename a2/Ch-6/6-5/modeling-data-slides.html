Correlation Coefficient ($r$)

A graphing calculator can find the line of best fit and automatically calculate the correlation coefficient, $r$. It measures how well a data set is modeled by a given equation.

  • Positive correlation: When $x$ increases, $y$ increases.
  • Negative correlation: When $x$ increases, $y$ decreases.
  • No correlation: Data are randomly dispersed ($r$ is close to 0).
  • Perfect correlation: Data lie exactly on the regression line ($r = \pm 1$).
  • Strong correlation: $|r| \ge 0.8$; Weak correlation: $|r| \le 0.5$.
