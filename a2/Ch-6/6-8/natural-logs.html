Base e and Natural Logarithms

The number e is an irrational number roughly equal to 2.71828...

An exponential function with base e is called a natural base exponential function.

The inverse of this function is the natural logarithm, written as ln x:

loge x = ln x, x > 0

Most calculators have an LN key to find natural logarithms.

Example 1: Write Exponential Equations in Logarithmic Form

Convert each exponential equation into its equivalent natural logarithmic form:

a. ex = 8

→ loge 8 = x → ln 8 = x

b. e5 = x

→ loge x = 5 → ln x = 5

Example 2: Write Logarithmic Equations in Exponential Form

Convert each natural logarithmic equation into its equivalent exponential form:

a. ln x ≈ 0.7741

→ loge x ≈ 0.7741 → x ≈ e0.7741

b. ln 10 = x

→ loge 10 = x → 10 = ex

Example 3: Simplify Expressions with e and ln

Write 3 ln 10 − ln 8 as a single logarithm.

Step 1: Apply the Power Property of Logarithms.

= ln 103 − ln 8

Step 2: Use the Quotient Property of Logarithms.

= ln(103 / 8)

Step 3: Evaluate the fraction and simplify.

= ln 125

= ln 53

= 3 ln 5

Example 4: Solve Base e Equations

Solve 4e−2x − 5 = 3. Round to the nearest ten-thousandth.

Step 1: Isolate the exponential term.

4e−2x = 8

e−2x = 2

Step 2: Take the natural logarithm of each side and use the property ln ex = x.

ln e−2x = ln 2

−2x = ln 2

Step 3: Divide by −2 and evaluate with a calculator.

x = ln 2 / −2

x ≈ −0.3466

Example 5: Solve Natural Log Equations

Solve 3 ln 4x = 24. Round to the nearest ten-thousandth.

Step 1: Isolate the logarithmic term.

ln 4x = 8

Step 2: Write in exponential form (or raise each side to base e).

eln 4x = e8

4x = e8

Step 3: Divide each side by 4 and calculate.

x = e8 / 4

x ≈ 745.2395
