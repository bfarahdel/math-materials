Lesson 6-4: Logarithms

For b>0,b≠1, and x>0:

logb x = y ↔ by = x

The variable y is called the logarithm of x.

Example 1: Log to Exponential

a. log2 8 = 3 → 23 = 8

b. log4 (1/256) = -4 → 4-4 = 1/256

Example 2: Exponential to Log

a. 153 = 3375 → log15 3375 = 3

b. 41/2 = 2 → log4 2 = 1/2

Example 3: Evaluating log16 4

Step 1: Set to y: log16 4 = y

Step 2: Exponential: 4 = 16y

Step 3: Solve: 41 = (42)y → y = 1/2
