Common Logarithons

Base 10 logarithms are called common logarithms.

Common logarithms are usually written without the subscript 10:

log10 x = log x, x > 0

Most scientific calculators have a LOG key for evaluating common logarithms.

Example 1: Find Common Logarithms

Use a calculator to evaluate each expression to the nearest ten-thousandth:

a. log 5

KEYSTROKES: LOG 5 ENTER

Display: 0.6989700043 → log 5 ≈ 0.6990

b. log 0.3

KEYSTROKES: LOG 0.3 ENTER

Example 3: Solve Exponential Equations

Solve 4x = 19. Round to the nearest ten-thousandth.

Step 1: Take the common logarithm of each side.

log 4x = log 19

Step 2: Use the Power Property of Logarithms.

x log 4 = log 19

Step 3: Divide each side by log 4 and use a calculator.

x = log 19 / log 4

x ≈ 2.1240

Example 4: Solve Exponential Inequalities

Solve 35y < 7y − 2. Round to the nearest ten-thousandth.

Step 1a: Take the common logarithm of each side.

log 35y < log 7y − 2

Step 1b: Apply the Power Property of Logarithms to move the exponents forward.

5y log 3 < (y − 2) log 7

Step 2: Distribute log 7 and isolate the variable terms on the left.

5y log 3 < y log 7 − 2 log 7

5y log 3 − y log 7 < −2 log 7

Step 3: Factor out y and divide to solve.

y(5 log 3 − log 7) < −2 log 7

y < −2 log 7 / (5 log 3 − log 7)

{y | y < −1.0972}

Key Concept: Change of Base Formula

For all positive numbers a, b, and n, where a ≠ 1 and b ≠ 1:

loga n = logb n / logb a

Example: Changing base to 10

log3 11 = log10 11 / log10 3
